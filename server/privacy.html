CV Tree Privacy Policy

Last updated {{UPDATED}} · Published by {{PUBLISHER}}

In short: your CVs, cover letters and profile stay in your browser. Text is sent to an AI provider only when you click an AI button. Anonymous usage statistics (which features are used, never your content) help improve CV Tree, and you can turn them off in Settings.

What CV Tree stores, and where

Everything you put into CV Tree (your CVs and uploaded files, cover letters, profile, work history, skills, projects, settings and any API keys you add) is stored only in your browser's extension storage on your device. We don't have a copy. Uninstalling the extension deletes it. You can export a backup from Settings at any time.

What CV Tree reads on websites

To show the CV Tree button on job postings, the extension checks pages you visit for a job posting. This check happens on your device. Nothing from the page is sent anywhere unless you use an AI action on it. On LinkedIn, the profile page text is read only when you click "Import this profile". When you click "Attach to this page", CV Tree puts your chosen CV into that page's upload field; you still submit the form yourself.

Third parties

AI requests are processed by OpenRouter and, depending on the model, Anthropic or the provider of the model you chose, under their own privacy policies. GitHub sign-in is handled by GitHub. We don't sell data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone else.

Free AI limits and logs

For the free AI we keep, per random install ID and day, the number of requests, tokens used and cost, so we can apply daily limits and keep the service affordable. These records are kept for up to 12 months.

Usage statistics retention

Anonymous usage events are kept for up to 24 months and then deleted. Turning statistics off stops sending immediately and deletes anything not yet sent.

Your choices

  • Turn off usage statistics in Settings › Usage statistics.
  • Use your own AI key instead of the free AI, in Settings › AI provider.
  • Delete your data by removing it in the app or uninstalling the extension.
  • To ask about or delete the anonymous records for your install, send us the install ID shown in Settings.

Children

CV Tree isn't directed at children under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the extension.
