CV TREE: HOW TO INSTALL (about 2 minutes)
==========================================

CV Tree finds your best CV for any job posting, tailors it with AI, writes the
cover letter and attaches your CV to the application. Your CVs stay in your
browser.

Works in Chrome, Edge and Brave on Windows, Mac and Linux.

1. UNZIP this file into a folder you'll keep, for example
   Documents\CV Tree. Don't delete or move that folder later:
   the browser runs CV Tree from it.

2. OPEN the extensions page. Type this in the address bar and press Enter:
      Chrome or Brave:  chrome://extensions
      Edge:             edge://extensions

3. TURN ON "Developer mode" (a switch in the top-right corner;
   in Edge it's on the left).

4. CLICK "Load unpacked" and choose the folder from step 1
   (the one that contains manifest.json).

5. A welcome page opens. Follow it: upload your CVs (PDF or Word), and you're
   ready. The AI is free (30 requests a day), no key needed.

6. PIN IT: click the puzzle-piece icon next to the address bar, then the pin
   next to CV Tree.

USING IT
--------
Open any job posting (LinkedIn, Indeed, Greenhouse, Lever, Workday...).
A green CV Tree button appears in the bottom-right corner with a score for
your best CV. Click it to see your best CVs, tailor one, write a cover letter,
or attach your CV to the application form.

GOOD TO KNOW
------------
- The first AI request after a quiet spell can take up to a minute while the
  free server wakes up. After that it's quick.
- Chrome may sometimes say "Disable developer mode extensions". Click the X
  or "Keep" to keep CV Tree.
- Want unlimited AI? Get a free key at openrouter.ai and add it in
  CV Tree > Settings > AI provider > My OpenRouter key.
- To update: replace the folder's files with the new version, then click the
  circular reload arrow on CV Tree's card at chrome://extensions.
  Your CVs are kept.
